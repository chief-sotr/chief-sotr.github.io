People are rising into these frequencies and "co-creating with these higher harmonic frequencies and creating this strong invisible architecture of light from which new realities will be birthed."

### Pam's Exact Words
"I do believe absolutely that the Capricorn solstice was the tipping point and we've now entered a whole new episode for humanity that is really the very beginning of the birth of what is called homo luminous — that we become beings of light."
