## Topic 13: The Inner Radio Dial — Sovereignty & Co-Creating Reality

### Overview
Pam returns to one of her most important teachings: each person holds the exclusive authority to set their own internal frequency. External chaos — however intense — cannot dictate your inner state unless you give it that power. Reacting to outer events is a form of giving power away; responding from center is staying in your power.

"Stay in your sovereignty. Develop that sense of the inner radio dial. You have the authority — and only you have the authority — to turn that radio dial. And no one else. Only you. It doesn't matter what authority figure they are, how important they are in the world. Only you can turn your own radio dial and create your own frequency, your own reality, your own bliss, your own world."

"If you're reacting to outer events, you're actually then giving away your power. If you're considered and measured and come from a place of center, then you're coming from your power rather than scattering it."

"What is happening in our external world is less important than how you respond to them."
